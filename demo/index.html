How it works

  1. Deal — buyer creates the deal: seller, amount, deliverable URL (sha256-pinned), terms.
  2. Dispute — buyer or seller opens a dispute with a claim.
  3. Adjudication — GenLayer validators fetch the evidence URL, judge it against the terms with LLM consensus, and record a verdict.
  4. Settlement — the verdict drives the escrow ledger: DELIVERED releases to the seller, NOT_DELIVERED refunds the buyer.
